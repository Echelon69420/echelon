# THE ECHELON PROOF SYSTEM

## Physical Verification Architecture

The Echelon proof pipeline is built on a single premise: **Accountability requires authenticity.**

Traditional habit apps rely on self-reported checkmarks or passive uploads that can be backdated, manipulated, or simulated with artificial imagery. Echelon incorporates an uncompromising physical proof architecture designed to feel like accountability, not surveillance.

---

### How Proof Capture Operates

When an active protocol requires proof of completion, athletes enter the live capture viewfinder. The capture sequence enforces strict anti-tamper safeguards:

#### 1. Real-Time Camera Capture Only
Gallery imports, file uploads, and pre-recorded videos are strictly blocked. Every proof capture happens live through the optical camera sensor within the designated daily accountability window.

#### 2. Dynamic Randomized Gesture Challenges
To eliminate pre-rendered AI imagery and archived photographs, the system generates an unpredictable physical gesture prompt at the precise second of capture (e.g., *Touch your left ear*, *Raise three fingers in frame*, *Present peace sign to camera*). Because the required posture is unknown in advance, it cannot be pre-generated or rehearsed.

#### 3. Operating System Capture Protection
During proof capture, the application enforces system-level window security policies that inhibit external screen recording, OS-level screenshots, and unauthorized background display interception.

#### 4. Automated Liveness Verification
Advanced on-device optical intelligence analyzes camera frames in real time to verify that the capture reflects a live three-dimensional human subject rather than a printed photograph, computer screen, or synthetic projection.

#### 5. Burned Traceable Watermark
Every captured proof has the athlete handle, UTC timestamp, and gesture badge burned permanently into the image payload. Proof items remain completely traceable to the originator, protecting athlete integrity and community accountability.

#### 6. Ephemeral 24-Hour Purge
Echelon is an accountability platform, not an archive. All proof media is automatically and permanently purged from server infrastructure within 24 hours of submission. Your data is never sold, never retained indefinitely, and never converted into a permanent surveillance log.
