## What is Echelon?

ECHELON is the high-performance accountability platform engineered for individuals who refuse to negotiate with their potential.

Most productivity and fitness trackers are built around comfortable fiction: self-reported checkmarks that cannot be verified, soft participation badges, and cartoonish streak freezes. Echelon discards the honor system entirely. In its place stands an uncompromising verification engine that turns commitments into verified physical records.

If it cannot be proven, it does not count.

## The Three Operational Arenas

Echelon structures discipline into three high-stakes environments:

### 1. The Arena (Red)
Multi-athlete elimination protocols. Athletes commit to rigorous multi-week standards (e.g., daily 5:00 AM cold exposure, 100km monthly volume, or uninterrupted deep work sessions). Every day carries a strict submission deadline. Miss the window or fail verification, and you are immediately eliminated from the bracket. Only the disciplined remain.

### 2. The 1v1 Duel (Blue)
Direct, head-to-head combat between two athletes across a defined 30-day timeline. 
* **Concealed Scoring**: Daily scores and split times remain locked until Day 30. Athletes cannot pace themselves against an opponent who is lagging or ease up when ahead; you are forced to compete against the absolute standard every single day.
* **Live Telemetry Sync**: Real-time progress updates, telemetry status, and psychological trash talk exchange.

### 3. Solo Protocols & Life Goals (Green)
Personal long-term mastery structured across core life disciplines (Athletic, Intellectual, Professional, and Spiritual).
* **Discipline Gates**: High-tier milestones remain strictly locked until baseline challenges are completed and verified.
* **Granular Metrics**: Real-world athletic and mental accounting calibrated in canonical units: pace, power, volume, duration, and sets.

## The Unbreakable Proof Pipeline

Echelon features one of the most rigorous verification pipelines in consumer software, engineered to feel like accountability, not surveillance.

* **Live Camera Capture Only**: Gallery uploads and archived files are strictly blocked. All proof is captured live through the optical camera sensor.
* **Capture-Time Randomized Gestures**: The application generates an unpredictable physical gesture at the exact second of capture (e.g., *Touch your left ear*, *Raise three fingers in frame*). Unknown in advance, this dynamic prompt eliminates artificial pre-generation and synthetic media.
* **OS-Level Capture Protection**: Window-level security protocols prevent external screen recording, OS-level screenshots, and unauthorized background display capture.
* **Automated Liveness Verification**: Intelligent computer vision verifies that camera frames represent a physical, living human in real three-dimensional space, rejecting screens and paper printouts.
* **Burned Traceable Watermark**: Athlete `@handle` and UTC timestamps are permanently fused into every proof image for cryptographic provenance.
* **24-Hour Ephemeral Purge**: Echelon is not an archive. All proof media is automatically and permanently purged from server infrastructure within 24 hours of submission. Your physical records are never permanently retained, never commercialized, and never turned into a surveillance history.
