### The Agon Biometric Smart Ring

The **Agon Smart Ring** is the physical hardware anchor of the Echelon ecosystem. Forged from aerospace-grade Grade 5 titanium, it provides continuous physiological telemetry directly to your Echelon athlete profile.

#### Key Hardware Characteristics
* **Continuous Autonomic Monitoring**: High-frequency optical PPG sensors measuring heart rate variability (HRV), resting pulse, and physiological stress during high-intensity training.
* **Strain & Recovery Scoring**: Automated physical exertion validation that feeds directly into Echelon's verified effort engine.
* **Zero Screen Distraction**: Screen-free, distraction-free industrial design engineered to disappear on the finger while recording uncompromised biometrics.
* **Waterproof to 100M**: Tested in open water, cold plunge, and extreme physical environments.

3. **Reservation Status**: Athletes can track their real-time queue position and reservation status directly from the Echelon application.
