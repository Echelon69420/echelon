# ECHELON BRAND ARCHITECTURE

## Visual Identity, Color System, and Brand Philosophy

The Echelon aesthetic reflects brutalist luxury: sharp geometric forms, letterpress emboss dynamics, high-contrast typography, and an unapologetic visual weight.

---

### Master Color Palette

#### Primary Canvas and Surfaces
* **Obsidian Canvas (`#111111`)**: Deep black ground underpinning every screen, view, and layout.
* **Card Surface (`#1A1A1A`)**: Matte card stock elevation for containers, metrics, and modals.
* **Deboss Surface (`#161616`)**: Pressed container state reflecting physical depression and tactile weight.
* **Input Surface (`#222222`)**: Form fields, scroll dials, and telemetry meters.

#### Accent Palette
* **Echelon Red (`#C0392B`)**: The color of urgency, competitive Arena arenas, and mandatory proof prompts.
* **Echelon Gold (`#C9A84C`)**: XP points, luxury tier status, verified founder badges, and master milestones.
* **Echelon Blue (`#1A5FA8`)**: 1v1 Duel interfaces, system alerts, and information hierarchies.
* **Echelon Green (`#27AE60`)**: Solo protocols, verified checkmarks, and milestone completion.
* **Bone White (`#F7F2E8`)**: Off-white text and primary typographic hierarchy, replacing harsh digital whites.

---

### Typography Hierarchy

Echelon enforces a strict three-font typographical hierarchy designed for rapid data consumption and timeless authority:

1. **Cinzel**: Used exclusively for the master `ECHELON` wordmark and primary ceremonial tier milestones.
2. **Barlow Condensed**: The competitive workhorse. Used for all numbers, telemetry readouts, status chips, button labels, and section dividers in bold and black weights.
3. **Barlow**: Crisp geometric sans-serif for body descriptions, policy disclosures, and narrative briefings.

---

### The Tactile Emboss System

Unlike standard flat interfaces, Echelon elements utilize physical elevation values:
* **Emboss (Resting State)**: Subtle top highlight paired with deep cast shadow, lifting cards and actionable buttons off the dark canvas.
* **Deboss (Pressed / Inactive State)**: Inset shading creating an appearance of mechanical engagement when an athlete commits to an action or confirms an entry.

Zero cartoonish illustrations. Zero soft design patterns. Every pixel communicates focus and authority.
