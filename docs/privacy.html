Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026 • Last Updated: September 22, 2026

Echelon Athletics (“Echelon”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile applications across Android and iOS, our website, and associated backend services.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Directly Provide

  • Account Credentials: When registering, we collect your unique Callsign (username), email address, and a cryptographically salted hash of your password. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Athletic Proofs & Media: When completing protocols or 1v1 duels, you submit photo evidence of physical task execution. These images are transmitted over TLS 1.3 and stored in encrypted object storage.
  • Communications: Support requests, feedback, and correspondence sent directly to our team.

B. Information Automatically Collected

  • Activity & Performance Logs: Completion timestamps, streak counters, duel outcomes, leaderboard point balances, and challenge statuses.
  • Device & Technical Diagnostics: Operating system version, device model, application bundle identifier (com.echelon.athlete), IP address, and installation UUIDs.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Apple Push Notification service (APNs) or Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens required to route duel invitations and streak countdown reminders.

C. Purchase & Financial Data

When you subscribe to Echelon Black Tier, payments are processed directly by Apple App Store (StoreKit) or Google Play Store (Google Play Billing). We receive only an encrypted validation token and expiration timestamp. We never collect, view, or store payment card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly to operate and enforce the Echelon athletic platform:

  • Authenticating your session and maintaining account security.
  • Verifying uploaded photographic proofs to eliminate cheating and maintain leaderboard integrity.
  • Synchronizing real-time 1v1 duels and head-to-head showdowns.
  • Calculating daily streaks and arena rankings.
  • Routing essential transactional notifications.

Zero Advertising & Data Selling: Echelon does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party data brokers or advertising networks.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

For athletes residing in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, we process personal information under the following legal bases pursuant to Article 6 of the GDPR:

Purpose Legal Basis (GDPR Art. 6)
Account creation, protocol logging, and duel execution Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b))
Anti-cheat verification, server security, and diagnostics Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f))
Legal and tax compliance Legal Obligation (Art. 6(1)(c))

4. Data Retention & Erasure

We retain personal data only for as long as your account remains active. You hold the absolute right to delete your account and all associated data at any time directly within the app (Settings → Security → Delete Account) or via our Account Deletion Portal. Deletion immediately purges profile records, duel logs, and stored proof photographs.

5. Security Measures

All network traffic between mobile clients and our infrastructure is strictly encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3). Passwords are encrypted using modern salted cryptographic hashing algorithms (Argon2 / BCrypt). Cloud databases are isolated in private VPC networks with role-based access control.

6. Children's Privacy

Echelon is not intended for individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 where required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

7. Your Statutory Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA, you hold the right to access, rectify, port, or erase your personal information, or to restrict its processing. To exercise any statutory rights, submit an inquiry to our privacy officer at kashish.sharma1870@gmail.com.
