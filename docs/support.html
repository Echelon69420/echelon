Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do workout proof submissions work?

When you log an athletic protocol (e.g. 100 pushups or Morning Warrior), Echelon requires genuine photographic evidence of task completion taken directly within the app. Submissions are timestamped, encrypted, and attached to your daily record or duel verification feed.

2. How do I restore my Echelon Black Tier subscription?

If you switched devices or reinstalled the application:

  1. Open Echelon and navigate to the Black Tier Paywall or Account Settings.
  2. Tap the Restore Purchases button.
  3. The client securely queries Apple StoreKit or Google Play Billing to restore and validate your active entitlement token.

3. What if a duel opponent uploads fraudulent proof?

Echelon enforces zero tolerance for cheating. In any 1v1 duel view, tap the Report Proof button on the opponent's submission. A moderator will inspect the cryptographic metadata and photo contents. Confirmed fraudulent submissions lead to immediate duel forfeiture, streak reset, and potential account suspension.

4. How do streaks work across different timezones?

Streaks are computed relative to your local device timezone at the moment of registration. When traveling across international date lines, protocols must still be fulfilled before midnight in your active operational zone.

5. How do I request account and data deletion?

Navigate to Settings → Security → Delete Account in the mobile app, or review our detailed Account Deletion Guide.
