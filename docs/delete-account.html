Account Deletion & Data Erasure

Compliance with Google Play Data Safety & Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v)

At Echelon, we respect your right to total data sovereignty. You have the absolute right to permanently delete your account, Callsign, workout history, and proof media at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can execute immediate, irreversible account deletion directly inside the Echelon mobile application on iOS and Android:

01

Open Account Settings

Launch the Echelon app, tap your avatar in the top right corner to open your Athlete Profile, and tap the Settings (Gear) icon.

02

Select Security

Under the Security & Compliance section, select Delete Account.

03

Confirm Permanent Purge

Review the warning summary and confirm deletion. The client issues an authenticated request to permanently delete your profile, immediately revoking all session tokens and wiping your local application data.

Method 2: Web & Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you may submit an email deletion request directly to our Data Protection Officer:

Email: support@echelon-athlete.com
Subject Line: Echelon Account Deletion Request
Required Details: Your registered email address and your active Callsign.

Once your identity is verified, your account and all associated records will be purged within 24 hours.

What Data is Permanently Destroyed?

Data Category Purge Effect Upon Deletion
User Profile & Callsign Permanently wiped from database. Callsign is released.
Authentication Credentials Hashed passwords and active JWT session tokens revoked and destroyed.
Proof Photographs All uploaded workout and challenge photo media permanently deleted from object storage.
Duels & Streaks Matchmaking records unlinked and streak counters permanently reset.
Push Notification Tokens Device APNs and FCM tokens unregistered and deleted.
